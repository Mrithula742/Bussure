# BusSure — Datasets and Research Papers

## Quick Overview
- 10 research papers selected: 6 PDFs uploaded, 4 available through links below.
- 3 datasets uploaded: Chennai MTC, Astana and synthetic crowding data.
- Crowding data contains 3,000 simulated ticket records, not official Chennai One transactions.
